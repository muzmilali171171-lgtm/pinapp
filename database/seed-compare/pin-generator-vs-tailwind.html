Pin Generator and Tailwind both focus heavily on Pinterest, but they solve different halves of the same problem. Pin Generator is a Shopify-focused app that turns your product catalog into pins automatically. Tailwind is a distribution and scheduling specialist that assumes the pin already exists. Understanding that split matters more than picking a "winner" between them.

Pricing

Pin Generator is distributed as a Shopify app with credit-based tiers — entry plans generally sit in the $17–30/month range for a few hundred credits and roughly 10 pins/day of automated posting, scaling up through higher tiers with more credits and daily volume as you pay more. Because it's Shopify-specific, it's really only relevant if your store already runs on that platform.

Tailwind's pricing restructured around a Pinterest-first model: a genuine free tier, then Pro at $29.99/month ($17.99/month billed annually), Advanced around $54.99/month, and Max at $99.99/month — priced per connected Pinterest account.

Pros and Cons

P
Pin Generator — best for Shopify stores with a full product catalog. It genuinely automates the creation step for e-commerce products, which most schedulers don't touch. The trade-off: it's design-only — no smart scheduling, no analytics depth, and no real presence outside Shopify.
T
Tailwind — best as the distribution layer. SmartSchedule, SmartLoop, and detailed analytics handle everything after a pin exists. The trade-off: its own design tool (Tailwind Create) is basic, and it doesn't read your site or catalog to generate pins on its own.

Creation vs. Distribution

The two tools are genuinely complementary rather than competing head-to-head: Pin Generator handles turning products into pin images, Tailwind handles getting those pins seen at the right times with recycling and community reach layered on top. Running both together for a Shopify store costs somewhere in the $45–60/month range combined — reasonable if e-commerce is your whole business, less so if you're not on Shopify at all, since Pin Generator's usefulness disappears outside that platform.

Where WebToPin fits in

WebToPin isn't limited to Shopify — it works from any website's existing pages, with AI writing the titles and descriptions, automatic board matching, and scheduling handled in one place, without needing a second tool for distribution. Plans start free, with paid tiers from $20/month.

Verdict

If you run a Shopify store and specifically need product images turned into pins with minimal effort, Pin Generator solves a real, narrow problem well. If you want intelligent scheduling, recycling, and analytics for pins you already have, Tailwind is the stronger specialist. For a platform-agnostic, all-in-one option that handles both creation and scheduling from any website — not just a Shopify catalog — that's the gap a tool like WebToPin is built to close.
